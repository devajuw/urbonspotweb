How It Works

1. List Your Spot

Users can list their parking spots by providing details such as location, availability, and pricing.

↓

2. Explore Parking

Drivers can explore available parking spots on the map and choose the one that suits their needs.

↓

3. Reserve & Pay

Drivers can reserve a spot and make payments securely through the platform.

↓

4. Navigate & Park

Get directions to the reserved parking spot and park your vehicle hassle-free.
